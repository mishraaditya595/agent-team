# Ideation modes

Adapted from gstack's CEO review scope modes (garrytan/gstack, MIT).

## `expand` — find the bigger product
Ask: what is the 10x version of what this product already does? What adjacent job do users hire it for? Good for products with traction that feel small. Ideas may be M/L effort.

## `hold` — polish what exists (default)
Ask: where do users get stuck, confused or slowed down today? What's half-built? Good for most weeks. Ideas should mostly be S/M effort.

## `reduce` — cut scope or complexity
Ask: what could we remove, merge or simplify without users losing value? What costs more to maintain than it returns? Good before a launch or when the product feels heavy. Ideas are often S effort and remove code.
