# No AI tells

Adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `copywriting` (AI-tells reference) and `social` (MIT). Readers scroll past copy that reads as generated, and stop trusting its claims. Run every draft against this list before you hand it over; rewrite anything that fails.

## Never write

- **The contrast reveal** — "Not X. Y.", "It's not X, it's Y", "Not just X, but Y", "Less X, more Y". State Y directly with its reason.
- **Negation lists** — "No setup, no templates, no waiting." Say what does happen, in the order it happens. One plain absence next to a call to action ("No card required") is fine.
- **Trailing pile-ons** — a full claim, then a comma and more restating clauses ("…, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks"). End the sentence at the claim; give a real extra fact its own sentence.
- **Self-answered questions and colon reveals** — "The result? 3x faster." "The best part: it learns." Say it.
- **Stock phrases** — "excited to announce", "say goodbye to", "X, reimagined", "unlock the power of", "take it to the next level", "here's the thing", "game-changer", "seamless", "elevate", "delve".
- **Em dashes in posts** — use a period or a line break. (Fine in long-form blog prose, sparingly.)
- **Broetry** — one short sentence per line for a whole post. Write paragraphs; break lines where a person would pause.
- **Engagement bait** — "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Let that sink in.", "Read that again." End on the point, or on a real question you want answered.
- **Manufactured vulnerability** — "I'll be honest, I was terrified…" with no real story behind it.
- **Emoji as bullets** — 🚀 ✅ 💡 leading every line.

Keep fragments and lists of three to one per piece.

## Always

- One specific fact, example or number per claim — and only facts from the spec, the PR, `project.md` or what the owner gave you. No invented metrics, users, quotes or testimonials, and no hedged versions of them.
- Write in the product's voice from `project.md` `## Positioning & voice`.
- Read it aloud once. If a sentence sounds like an ad nobody would say to a friend, rewrite it.
