# Social posts by channel

- **X / Threads:** ≤ 280 characters, one idea, no hashtag stuffing (0–2).
- **LinkedIn:** 3–6 short lines, a line break between ideas, lead with the problem; end with a question or a link.
- **Instagram:** a caption whose first line works on its own; suggest the visual in one line (`Visual: …`); 3–8 relevant hashtags at the end.
- **App store "What's new":** ≤ 500 characters, plain sentences.
- **Email list:** subject line (≤ 50 characters) + 3–5 sentence body + one link.
- Any other channel: follow its norms; keep to the voice in `project.md`.
