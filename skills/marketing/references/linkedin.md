# LinkedIn

Adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `social` (platform guide and hooks; MIT).

LinkedIn rewards a person telling a professional story with a lesson in it; it buries ads, corporate speak and outbound links. Write as the builder (the owner), in first person, unless `project.md` says the product posts as a brand.

## Shape

- **The first line is everything** — it's all that shows before "see more". Use a specific hook: a result with a number you actually have, a short story opening ("Last week a user asked…"), a contrarian view you can back up, or a how-to promise. Never a vague teaser.
- **Body:** about 1,200–1,500 characters. The problem a real person had → what you built or learned → one concrete detail (a screenshot, a step, a before/after) → the lesson for the reader.
- Short paragraphs with a blank line between them — paragraphs, not one sentence per line.
- **Put the link in the first comment**, not the post body (links in the body cut reach). Draft that comment too.
- End on the point, or one real question you want people to answer. No "Agree?"
- 0–3 hashtags, only if they're genuinely used in the niche. Tag people only when they were really involved.

## Formats

- **Text post** — the default.
- **Document (carousel) post** — when the content is steps or a list; reuse the Instagram carousel shapes in `instagram.md`, slides in landscape or square.
- **Short build-in-public update** — after a release: what was annoying before, what's different now, one thing that was hard to build.

## Output

```
### Post
<first line>
<body>
### First comment
<link + one line>
### Alternative first lines
1. … 2. …
```
